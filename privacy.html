Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 July 2026

GrowSense helps parents and guardians understand a child's growth. Because that means handling a child's health information, we treat this data with particular care. This policy explains what we collect, why, where it lives, who can see it, and the choices you have. If anything here is unclear, email us at contact@growsense.life.

Who holds an account. GrowSense accounts are created and managed by a parent or legal guardian on behalf of a child. The adult account holder is in control of the child's information at all times and can export or delete it whenever they choose.

1. What we collect

We only collect what the app needs to do its job. You decide how much to enter — most fields are optional.

Account

  • Your email address and the sign-in method you choose (Google, Apple, or email and password).

Child profile

  • The child's first name (or a nickname you choose), date of birth, and biological sex — used to place measurements on the correct WHO reference and DRI targets.
  • Optionally, parents' heights and ages, used only to estimate a mid-parental target-height range.

Growth and daily logs

  • Measurements you record: height, weight, and the BMI derived from them.
  • Daily nutrition, activity, and sleep entries you log.

Medical records (only if you choose to use these tools)

  • Illness history, medications, lab results, and puberty-staging notes you enter.
  • Bone-age X-ray images you upload for skeletal-maturity tracking.

Connected devices (optional)

  • If you connect a wearable such as Fitbit or Google Health, we receive the sleep and activity figures needed to fill those logs. You can disconnect at any time.

2. How we use it

  • To calculate and show your child's growth percentiles, target-height range, readiness score, and trends.
  • To provide the AI coach's template answers, which fill in your child's own numbers.
  • To let you review, correct, and export your family's history.
  • To keep your account secure and functioning.

We do not sell your data, show advertising, or use your child's health information to train third-party advertising or profiling systems.

3. Children's data

GrowSense is designed to be used by an adult about a child. We do not knowingly allow a child to create their own account. The information you enter about your child is health information, and we handle it only for the purposes above and only for as long as you keep it in your account. You may delete any child's record, or your whole account, at any time (see Your rights).

Bone-age X-ray images deserve special mention: they are stored solely so you can keep a serial history and, if you choose, share it with a clinician. They are never used for any other purpose.

4. Who can see it

  • You. Your family's data is private to your account by default.
  • People you explicitly invite. If you grant access to a doctor or a researcher, they can see the records you share, and only those. You can revoke access at any time.
  • No one else. We do not share your data with advertisers, data brokers, or unrelated third parties.

5. Service providers

A small number of trusted providers help us run the app. They process data only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, and secure storage.
  • Google and Apple — only if you choose their sign-in.
  • Fitbit / Google Health — only if you connect a wearable.
  • GitHub Pages — serves the app's web files (static hosting).

6. Where it's stored

Your data is stored in our Supabase project hosted in the Singapore region. Access is restricted at the database level so that each account can reach only its own records.

7. Security

  • Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
  • Row-level security rules enforce that you can read and write only your own family's records.
  • Sign-in is handled by established providers; we never see your Google or Apple password.

No online service can promise perfect security, but we work to protect your data and to limit what we hold to what the app genuinely needs.

8. Retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete a record or your account, it is removed from our live database. Some day-level logs may be pruned automatically after a period of inactivity to keep the app fast; your measurement history and account are kept until you delete them.

9. Your rights

You are in control of your family's data:

  • Access & export — view your history in the app and export it.
  • Correct — edit any entry at any time.
  • Delete — remove any record, or use Delete account in Account & settings to erase everything.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional legal rights (for example under Thailand's PDPA or the EU/UK GDPR). To exercise any of them, email contact@growsense.life and we will respond.

10. International users

GrowSense is offered in several countries across Asia, the Middle East, and beyond. Wherever you use it, we apply the practices in this policy. If your local law grants you stronger protections, those protections apply to you.

11. Changes to this policy

If we make a meaningful change, we will update the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, tell you in the app. Continuing to use GrowSense after a change means you accept the updated policy.

12. Contact

Questions, requests, or concerns about your privacy? Email contact@growsense.life. We read every message.
