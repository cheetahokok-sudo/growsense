# GS-002 · References

> **Rule (inherited from the coach-library no-fabrication rule):** a citation
> only moves from *Pending* to *Verified* after its title, authors, journal,
> year, and identifier resolve to a real record. Anything that can't be
> verified is **cut, not guessed.**

## ⚠️ Verification story — the entire draft list was fabricated

The author's original 28-reference list was **systematically fabricated**: the
PMIDs were real numbers pointing to **completely unrelated papers**. Confirmed
mismatches included:

- "Palmert & Dunkel, Delayed puberty" (PMID 22256806) → *bone-density testing in
  older women*.
- "Tanner, height-velocity & adult-height prediction, 1970" (PMID 978275) →
  *thiamine on the crayfish giant axon*.
- "Martin, use of bone age" (PMID 22065188) → *youth injury epidemic*.
- "Larson, deep learning bone age" (PMID 30799684) → *EGFR cancer imaging*.
- "Silventoinen twin study" (PMID 20687837) → *arterolane malaria trial*.
- "Wright & Cheetham parental height" (PMID 15781967) → *neuroscience award notice*.
- "Hermanussen & Cole target height" (PMID 14671466) → *contraceptive pill in teens*.
- "Satoh bone age" (PMID 26019789) → *sickle-cell adrenal disease*.

Only a handful were genuine (Wood 2014, Yengo 2018, Yang/Visscher 2010,
NCD-RisC 2016). **The list below was rebuilt from scratch** — every claim
re-sourced to the real paper and PMID-verified, with newer corroborating work
added per request.

## Verified — checked against PubMed, 2026-07-12

### A. Genetics & heritability
1. Silventoinen K, et al. Heritability of adult body height: eight-country twin study. *Twin Res.* 2003. PMID: 14624724. *(60–80%.)*
2. Yang J, … Visscher PM. Common SNPs explain a large proportion of the heritability for human height. *Nat Genet.* 2010;42(7):565–569. PMID: 20562875. *(~45% by all common SNPs. Draft mistitled this as "assumption-free… Visscher.")*

### G. Improved & extended target-height method (matches the app)
23. Zeevi D, Ben Yehuda A, Nathan D, Zangen D, Kruglyak L. Accurate prediction of children's target height from their mid-parental height. *Children (Basel).* 2024;11(8):916. PMID: 39201851. *(23 large families; raw MPH runs ~2.7 cm low, explains 36% variance; three corrections — nonlinear parental age, multiplicative sex, regression to the mean — raise it to 40%; real residual SD ≈4.5 cm sons / 4.2 cm daughters. This is the method `target-height.js` / `growth_math.dart` implement.)*
24. Sorkin JD, Muller DC, Andres R. Longitudinal change in height of men and women: … the Baltimore Longitudinal Study of Aging. *Am J Epidemiol.* 1999;150(9):969–977. PMID: 10547143. *(Age-shrinkage: begins ~30; 30→70 ≈ 3 cm men / 5 cm women; by 80 ≈ 5/8 cm.)*
25. Galton F. Regression towards mediocrity in hereditary stature. *J Anthropol Inst.* 1886;15:246–263. *(Classic origin of regression to the mean.)*

**App note:** the article's §3 describes GrowSense's *actual* target-height
feature — the validated Zeevi corrections (parents-only) plus the **exploratory,
clearly-labelled** extended-family estimate (grandparents/aunts/uncles/siblings
weighted by coefficient of relationship, blended 70/30 with the validated
result). The naive "3-generation auto-substitution of short parents" was
deliberately **rejected** in the code and is NOT claimed in the article — ordinary
family height variation is normal, not an error to overwrite.
