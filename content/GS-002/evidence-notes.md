## Framing guardrails honoured
- **Prediction is a range, not a ceiling** — stated in the executive summary,
  §3, §10, and §13; every prediction claim is bounded with real margins of error
  (refs 8, 9).
- **No "increase height" claim** — §12 is explicitly *remove obstacles / protect
  potential*, not add centimetres.
- **AI framed as a second opinion, not diagnosis** (§7) — matches the app's
  bone-age module positioning.
- **Numbers are sourced or labelled illustrative** — the reliability-by-age
  table is qualitative, anchored to ref 9's real ±cm figures.

## The lesson
This draft is the reason the verification gate exists. ~10 of the 28 draft
PMIDs that were checked pointed to unrelated papers (malaria, crayfish axons,
cancer imaging…). Publishing it unverified would have put fabricated citations
on the "every claim sourced" flagship article. Rebuilt 100% from verified
sources.
