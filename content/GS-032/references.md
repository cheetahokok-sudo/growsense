# GS-032 · References

> **Rule (inherited from the coach-library no-fabrication rule):** a citation
> only moves from *Pending* to *Verified* after its title, authors, journal,
> year, and identifier resolve to a real record. Anything that can't be
> verified is **cut, not guessed.** Only the Verified block may appear in
> published articles, graphics, or scripts.

## Corrected during verification — draft mislabels

The draft's PMIDs pointed to **real, relevant papers**, but three were labelled
with the wrong author/journal. Do not reintroduce the wrong labels:

- "Voortman T, *Am J Clin Nutr* 2016, 104(4):1154–1163" → actually **Braun et
  al., *J Nutr* 2016;146(11):2361–2367** (ref 2).
- "Jen V et al., PMID 30869114" → that PMID is **Switkowski** (ref 1); the draft
  double-listed the same paper. Dropped the duplicate.
- "Hoppe C, *Nutrients* 2021, PMID 34333391" → actually **Grenov et al.,
  *Growth Horm IGF Res* 2021** (ref 7).

## Note on the 1.0–1.5 g/kg "active child" range (§5)
The **RDA floor** (~0.95 g/kg/d) is cited to IOM (ref 11). The higher
**1.0–1.5 g/kg operational range** is framed in-text as common dietitian
practice guidance, **not** as a specific cited trial value — do not attach a
citation to it that hasn't been individually verified.
