## Framing guardrails honoured
- **No "protein increases height" claim.** The thesis throughout is *fuel to
  reach genetic potential, not a lever to exceed it* — the executive summary,
  §1 "construction rule", §6, and §7 all state this explicitly.
- **Leucine** is described as a trigger for **muscle** protein synthesis
  (accurate), not miscast as a direct bone-lengthening agent.
- **Honest on plant vs. animal:** animal/dairy has a measurable IGF-1 edge (refs
  6, 7, 9), *and* varied plant diets reach the same growth outcomes (ref 10) —
  both stated, neither oversold.

## Claims deliberately NOT independently cited
- **"1.0–1.5 g/kg for active children"** — framed as dietitian practice
  guidance; the cited value is the IOM RDA floor (~0.95 g/kg, ref 11). See
  references.md note.
- **Food protein amounts** (egg ~6–7 g, Greek yogurt ~10–15 g) — standard
  food-composition values, not a research claim.
