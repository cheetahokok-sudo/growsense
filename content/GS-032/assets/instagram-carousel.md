# GS-032 · Instagram Carousel (10 slides)

Format 4:5 (1080×1350). Voice: calm, credible, myth-busting but warm. The job is
to kill the "more protein = taller" belief without shaming any diet. Stat slides
carry a small "ref N" (numbers match `references.md`).

---

**Slide 1 — Hook**
"Feed them more protein and they'll grow taller."
It's the most common growth myth.
Here's what protein *actually* does. 🧵
_(GrowSense · the science, minus the hype)_

**Slide 2 — The one-line truth**
Protein is **fuel**, not a height dial.
It helps a child reach the height their **genes** set —
it can't push them past it. _(ref 1, 5)_

**Slide 3 — Blueprint vs. bricks**
Genes = the blueprint.
Protein = the bricks.
No bricks → building stalls. But endless extra bricks
don't make the house taller than the plan. _(ref 5)_

**Slide 4 — Protein 101**
Protein = chains of **amino acids.**
9 are "essential" — the body can't make them,
so they must come from food *every day.*

**Slide 5 — Quality > quantity**
Stunted kids aren't just low on calories —
they're low on **essential amino acids.** _(ref 15)_
Variety and quality beat sheer grams.

**Slide 6 — Complete vs. combine**
Egg, milk, soy = complete.
Rice + beans = complete *together.*
(Grains lack lysine, legumes lack methionine — they fix each other.)

**Slide 7 — How much? (less than you think)**
Ages 4–8: ~19 g/day
Ages 9–13: ~34 g/day
A 20 kg child: ~20–30 g — one egg, a cup of milk,
a piece of fish. No powder needed. _(ref 11)_

**Slide 8 — The milk question**
Does milk make kids taller?
A little — about **0.4 cm/yr per glass** —
and mostly in kids who were short on quality protein. _(ref 12)_
Not a height lever.

**Slide 9 — 3 honest myth-busters**
❌ Protein powder for a healthy kid
❌ "Just feed more" (adds weight, not height)
❌ Protein harms bones (it *helps* them) _(ref 16)_

**Slide 10 — Soft CTA**
Stop chasing grams. Support the **whole system** —
sleep, food, activity — so the body is ready to grow.
GrowSense sets protein targets to your child's own age & weight.
_(Save this · Follow for the science, minus the fear)_
