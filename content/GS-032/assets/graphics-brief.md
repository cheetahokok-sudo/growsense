# GS-032 · Graphics Briefs

Four graphics per article. **Brand system:** deep-green `#0E2A20` grounds,
accent `#2F6B4F`, measured-blue `#2A5C8A`, estimated-gold `#9C7A3D`, flag-red
`#A23B3B` (alerts only). Headings Fraunces, body Inter (Sarabun for Thai).
Flat, calm, no fear imagery. Every graphic carries a small "GrowSense · GS-032"
credit and, where a stat appears, its reference number from `references.md`.

## 1. Hero graphic (article header, 1600×900 + 1:1 social crop)
- **Concept:** a warm, appetizing flat-lay of child-friendly protein foods —
  eggs, a glass of milk, yogurt, a fillet of fish, tofu cubes, lentils, beans,
  a small handful of nuts — arranged on a light table with a soft green accent.
- **Headline overlay space:** upper-left kept clean for "Protein & height."
- **Tone:** abundance from *ordinary* food (not supplements). No powders, no
  scoops, no "muscle" imagery.

## 2. Blueprint-vs-bricks graphic (1:1 + 4:5)
- **Concept:** split panel. Left: a house *blueprint* labeled "Genes = the
  final height." Right: a pile of bricks labeled "Protein = the materials."
  A small caption bar: "No bricks → building stalls. Endless extra bricks →
  not a taller house."
- **Footnote:** "Excess protein ≠ extra height — ref 5."
- **Guardrail:** the honest core message of the article, stated visually.

## 3. Protein-quality scoreboard (1:1 + 4:5)
- **Concept:** a clean tiered list (DIAAS framing):
  Top — egg, milk, dairy, whey · Strong — soy/tofu, meat, fish · Combine —
  lentils/beans (low methionine) + grains/rice (low lysine).
- **Visual hook:** a "rice + beans = ✓ complete" pairing badge.
- **Footnote:** "Protein quality (DIAAS) — ref 17."

## 4. "A day of enough protein" graphic (1:1 + 4:5, save-worthy)
- **Concept:** two simple columns for a ~20 kg child, each adding to ~26 g:
  *Omnivore* — 1 egg (6 g) + cup of milk (8 g) + fish/chicken (12 g).
  *Plant-based* — dhal (9 g) + tofu (10 g) + rice & nuts (7 g).
- **Bottom band:** "~20–30 g/day — from food, no powder needed."
- **Footnote:** "RDA — ref 11."
- **Guardrail:** reassuring and practical; no gram-anxiety, no supplement.

## Production notes
- Provide each in light + dark variants where used on web.
- Thai versions use Sarabun and localized copy from the translation pass.
- Never overstate: no "+X cm" claims; no image implying powders/supplements are
  needed for a healthy child.
