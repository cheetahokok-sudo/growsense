### A. Milk & linear growth
1. de Beer H. Dairy products and physical stature: systematic review and
   meta-analysis of controlled trials. *Econ Hum Biol.* 2012;10(3):299–309.
   PMID: 21890437. *(~0.4 cm/y per ~245 mL/d; biggest in undernourished.)*
2. Marshall TA, et al. Higher longitudinal milk intakes are associated with
   increased height in a birth cohort followed for 17 years. *J Nutr.* 2018.
   PMID: 29924327. *(+0.39 cm per 236 mL/d — matches de Beer.)*
3. Herber C, Bogler L, Subramanian SV, Vollmer S. Association between milk
   consumption and child growth for children aged 6–59 months. *Sci Rep.* 2020.
   PMID: 32317668. *(668k children LMIC; ↓stunting ~1.9 pp; notes SES confounding.)*
4. Wiley AS. Does milk make children grow? … NHANES 1999–2002. *Am J Hum Biol.*
   2005. PMID: 15981182. *(No height effect 5–11 y; modest in adolescents.)*
5. Mayer-Davis E, Leidy H, Mattes R, et al. Beverage consumption and growth,
   size, body composition… a systematic review. USDA NESR; 2020. PMID: 35349233.

### C. Bone: density is not length
9. de Lamas C, et al. Effects of dairy product consumption on height and bone
   mineral content in children: a systematic review of controlled trials. *Adv
   Nutr.* 2019. PMID: 31089738.
10. Hidayat K, Zhang LL, Rizzoli R, et al. The effects of dairy product
    supplementation on bone health indices in children aged 3 to 18 years:
    meta-analysis of RCTs. *Adv Nutr.* 2023;14(5):1187–1196. PMID: 37414219.
    *(+~25 g bone mineral but only +0.21 cm height — the density paradox.)*
