The audit trail. Every load-bearing claim in `article.md` maps to a verified
reference in `references.md`. Reassurance claims ("most short children are
healthy") are framed as the clinical consensus, sourced to the review/guideline
literature — never as a bare assertion.

## Framing guardrails honoured
- **No "increases height" claim.** Section 8 is explicitly *remove obstacles*,
  not *add centimetres*; weight-bearing activity "strengthens bone, not
  lengthens it."
- **Reassurance is sourced, not asserted.** The "most short children are
  healthy" thesis rests on the review/guideline consensus (refs 1, 5).
- **Percentiles are references, not verdicts** — stated in-text and in the
  closing disclaimer.
