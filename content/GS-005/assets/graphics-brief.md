# GS-005 · Graphics Briefs

Four graphics per article. **Brand system:** deep-green `#0E2A20` grounds,
accent `#2F6B4F`, measured-blue `#2A5C8A`, estimated-gold `#9C7A3D`, flag-red
`#A23B3B` (alerts only). Headings Fraunces, body Inter (Sarabun for Thai).
Flat, calm, no fear imagery. Every graphic carries a small "GrowSense · GS-005"
credit and, where a stat appears, its reference number from `references.md`.

## 1. Hero graphic (article header, 1600×900 + 1:1 social crop)
- **Concept:** a shorter child standing **confidently** among taller peers, with
  a soft growth chart behind showing a **steady low percentile line** (tracking
  its channel, not falling). Warm, calm, dignified — the child is the subject,
  not the problem.
- **Headline overlay:** "Is my child too short? When to actually worry"
- **Tone:** reassurance. The visual message is *steady + healthy*, never a race
  or a deficit. No "taller = better" cue.

## 2. Velocity graphic — "the number that matters" (1:1 + 4:5)
- **Concept:** two mini growth lines side by side.
  Left: "Short — but healthy" — low line climbing **+5 cm/yr** (accent-green).
  Right: "Taller — but stalling" — higher line flattening to **+2 cm/yr**
  (gold/flag treatment on the flattening segment).
- **Headline:** "Velocity beats height."
- **Footnote:** "Growth velocity — ref 6."
- **Guardrail:** the point is *trajectory*, not that short = bad.

## 3. Two-patterns graphic — familial vs. late bloomer (1:1 + 4:5)
- **Concept:** clean two-column comparison card:
  **Familial short stature** — short parents · normal velocity · bone age = age ·
  on-time puberty · shorter healthy adult.
  **Constitutional delay (late bloomer)** — late-puberty family history · normal
  velocity · **delayed bone age** · late puberty · usually catches up.
- **Headline:** "Two normal reasons a healthy child is short."
- **Footnote:** "ref 1, 8."

## 4. Red-flags graphic — "when to see a doctor" (1:1 + 4:5, save-worthy)
- **Concept:** a calm checklist card (not alarmist), four items with small
  icons: downward percentile crossing · velocity < ~4 cm/yr · no puberty by
  13 (girls) / 14 (boys) · slow growth **+** feeling unwell.
- **Bottom band (the reassuring rule):** "Well and growing steadily → watch.
  Slowing, crossing lines, or unwell → see a doctor."
- **Footnote:** "ref 2, 7, 15."
- **Guardrail:** frame as *empowerment*, never fear; red used sparingly for the
  checklist ticks only.

## Production notes
- Provide each in light + dark variants where used on web.
- Thai versions use Sarabun and localized copy from the translation pass.
- Never overstate: no "+X cm" claims, and no image implying short = unhealthy.
