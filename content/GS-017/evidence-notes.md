## Thesis
The tallest child in the class isn't always the tallest adult. Early puberty
**speeds growth up while shortening its duration** — earlier estrogen → earlier
growth-plate fusion → shorter runway. So a child can be taller *today*, growing
faster *today*, yet finish *shorter* than genetics predicted. Reframes "is my
child growing fast?" into "how long can they keep growing?" — the [[GS-019]] runway
idea applied to puberty timing. On-brand: turns a reassuring-looking pattern
(tall early) into an honest, sometimes-cautionary one, without fear-mongering.

## Load-bearing claims → refs
- Puberty raises velocity (7–9 girls / 8–10 boys cm-yr) via GH/IGF-1/sex steroids → r1.
- Puberty simultaneously accelerates bone-age/fusion → r2, r3.
- **Estrogen closes plates in both sexes** (boys via aromatase; AI evidence) → r3, r4, r5.
- Bone age = biological maturity > birthdays → r2.
- Definitions: girls <8, boys <9; premature thelarche = benign variant → r6, r7.
- Untreated CPP → compromised final adult height (the classic arc) → r8.
- Idiopathic in girls / more pathology in boys; work-up (GnRH stim, MRI select) → r6, r10.
- Obesity ↔ earlier puberty (leptin permissive on HPG) = strongest modifiable factor → r9.
- GnRHa preserves (not boosts) height; benefit depends on age/BA/velocity/target;
  earliest-treated gain most → r10, r11, r12, r13.
- Blockers + fertility: puberty resumes, reassuring so far → r10, r13.

## Honesty choices
- GnRHa framed as **preserving** height lost to early fusion, NOT a height booster
  (callout) — matches the consensus + brand.
- "Early puberty is not always disease" kept as its own point — avoid medicalising
  normal variation; goal = find the minority losing height / with pathology.
- The draft's "GrowSense Runway Model" weight table was cut as pseudo-precise
  (same call as GS-019); folded into the CTA honestly.
- Food-myth claims (chicken/milk/soy/EDC/COVID) **split out** — they need their own
  PMID sourcing and are a marquee Myths-cluster pillar for Asian markets.

## The reference story

were fabricated** (pointed to AIDS microbiota, TB mice, Crohn's, tricuspid valve,
diet cost) — worse ratio than GS-019, incl. the SAME bad `26191508`. All
corrected/cut before ship (see references.md). Reinforces: verify every PMID.
