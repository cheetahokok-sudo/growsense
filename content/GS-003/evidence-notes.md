## Thesis
There is no single "normal" number for an age. A chart shows *position in
a distribution*; what matters is a coherent *trajectory*. The 50th is not
a target. The load-bearing, on-brand twist: **the chart you choose can
change the answer** — so an Asian child is not "small" just because a
Western-built curve says so. Reassurance, honestly sourced.
