# GS-001 · References

> **Rule (inherited from the coach-library no-fabrication rule):** a citation
> only moves from *Pending* to *Verified* after its title, authors, journal,
> year, and identifier resolve to a real record. Anything that can't be
> verified is **cut, not guessed.** Only the Verified block may appear in
> published articles, graphics, or scripts.

## Verified — checked against PubMed / publisher, 2026-07-11

1. **Van Cauter E, Plat L.** Physiology of growth hormone secretion during
   sleep. *J Pediatr.* 1996;128(5 Pt 2):S32–S37. PMID: 8627466.
   *(~70% of sleep GH pulses coincide with slow-wave sleep.)*
2. **Lango Allen H, et al.** Hundreds of variants clustered in genomic loci
   and biological pathways affect human height. *Nature.*
   2010;467(7317):832–838. PMID: 20881960.
3. **Silventoinen K, et al.** Heritability of adult body height: a comparative
   study of twin cohorts in eight countries. *Twin Res.* 2003;6(5):399–408.
   PMID: 14624724. *(Heritability ~0.68–0.84.)*
4. **Juul A.** The effects of oestrogens on linear bone growth. *Hum Reprod
   Update.* 2001;7(3):303–313. PMID: 11392373.
   *(Oestrogen drives the pubertal growth spurt and growth-plate fusion.)*
5. **Rogol AD, Clark PA, Roemmich JN.** Growth and pubertal development in
   children and adolescents: effects of diet and physical activity. *Am J
   Clin Nutr.* 2000;72(2 Suppl):521S–528S. PMID: 10919954.
   *(NOTE: the first draft misattributed this to "Endocrinol Metab Clin
   North Am. 2002" — wrong journal and year. Corrected here.)*
6. **Wit JM, Kamp GA, Oostdijk W.** Towards a rational and efficient
   diagnostic approach in children referred for growth failure to the general
   paediatrician. *Horm Res Paediatr.* 2019;91(4):223–240. PMID: 31195397.
   *(Basis for the "when to see a doctor" guidance, incl. bone-age screening.)*
7. **Brown KH, et al.** Effect of supplemental zinc on the growth and serum
   zinc concentrations of prepubertal children: a meta-analysis of randomized
   controlled trials. *Am J Clin Nutr.* 2002;75(6):1062–1071.
   *(Pooled height effect size ~0.35 SD.)*
8. **Greulich WW, Pyle SI.** Radiographic Atlas of Skeletal Development of the
   Hand and Wrist. 2nd ed. Stanford, CA: Stanford University Press; 1959.
   *(Reference standard for bone-age assessment.)*
