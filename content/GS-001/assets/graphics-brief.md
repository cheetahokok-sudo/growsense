# GS-001 · Graphics Briefs

Four graphics per article. **Brand system:** deep-green `#0E2A20` grounds,
accent `#2F6B4F`, measured-blue `#2A5C8A`, estimated-gold `#9C7A3D`, flag-red
`#A23B3B` (alerts only). Headings Fraunces, body Inter (Sarabun for Thai).
Flat, calm, no fear imagery. Every graphic carries a small "GrowSense ·
GS-001" credit and, where a stat appears, its reference number from
`references.md`.

## 1. Hero graphic (article header, 1600×900 + 1:1 social crop)
- **Concept:** two same-age children at different heights on a soft growth
  curve, with a subtle percentile band behind them — calm, warm, not
  competitive. No "taller = better" cue.
- **Headline overlay:** "Why do some children grow faster than others?"
- **Tone:** reassurance. The visual message is *normal variation*, not a race.

## 2. Growth-system graphic — the GH–IGF-1 axis (1:1 + 4:5)
- **Concept:** clean flow diagram, top to bottom:
  Pituitary → (GH pulses) → Liver → (IGF-1) → Growth plates → bone lengthens.
- **Style:** four labeled nodes on the deep-green ground, accent-green
  connectors, one short caption per node. No anatomy gore — schematic.
- **Footnote stat:** "~70% of sleep GH pulses occur in deep (N3) sleep — ref 1."

## 3. Bone-age graphic (1:1 + 4:5)
- **Concept:** side-by-side hand/wrist X-ray silhouettes labeled "Delayed bone
  age → more time to grow" and "Advanced bone age → shorter window," with a
  small calendar vs. skeleton icon contrasting chronological vs. bone age.
- **Footnote:** "Bone age is read from a hand/wrist X-ray — ref 8."
- **Guardrail:** frame as *information*, never as a verdict on the child.

## 4. Activity-evidence graphic (1:1 + 4:5)
- **Concept:** two columns — "Builds bone strength (high-impact, weight-
  bearing): basketball, volleyball, gymnastics, sprinting, rope-jumping" vs.
  "Great for heart & muscle (low-impact): swimming, cycling."
- **The honest headline (this is the differentiator):** "Exercise builds
  stronger bones — not taller ones."
- **Footnote:** "ref 5, 7."

## Production notes
- Provide each in light + dark variants where used on web.
- Thai versions use Sarabun and localized copy from the translation pass.
- Never overstate: no "+X cm" claims anywhere in any graphic.
