## Thesis
There is no single "world" or "Asian" growth chart. A child's percentile
depends on the reference chosen, and that reference is a decision. The
country-by-country tour makes the [[GS-003]] reassurance concrete: an
Asian child is not "small" because a Western-built curve says so.
