## The honest thesis (consistent with GS-001 / GS-005)
Exercise does **not** lengthen bones or add height beyond genetic potential — it
builds **stronger, denser bone** through mechanical loading, supports the growth
environment (sleep, appetite), and improves posture (how tall you *look*).
High-impact/weight-bearing > low-impact (swimming/cycling) for bone. Intense
elite training can *delay* maturation, but final height generally catches up to
the target. Frame as "stronger bones, not taller ones."
