## Framing guardrails honoured
- **No "exercise makes children taller" claim.** The thesis throughout is
  *stronger bones, not longer bones*; §3 states the distinction explicitly, and
  §5–8 debunk the height-hack myths (basketball / jumping / swimming / hanging /
  stretching).
- **The GrowSense tiers are labelled as a product framework**, informed by the
  impact literature (15, 6) — not presented as a published metric.
- **Torun (ref 4) is quarantined to the malnutrition context** and explicitly
  contrasted with well-fed children.
- **Intensive-training risk is attributed to under-fuelling (RED-S)**, with the
  honest note that final height is generally not compromised (ref 18).
- **Actionable + honest:** every "tomorrow morning" action is tied to evidence
  (jumping counts → 10–12; weekly targets → 5; impact ranking → 15).
