# GrowSense Growth Science — Content Roadmap

Year-one plan for the Evidence Library. **Goal:** become the trusted, honest
authority on childhood growth in our markets (Thailand-first), so organic
search compounds over several years.

## Operating principles

1. **Publish by search intent, not by teaching order.** Readers arrive from
   Google on their specific worry — they don't read GS-001→GS-050 in sequence.
   The 5 phases below organize the library; the *launch order* follows demand.
2. **Pillars, not thin posts.** Each cluster has one deep **pillar** article
   targeting the head keyword, plus a few **supporting** articles that
   interlink to it. Avoid multiple thin pages competing for the same query
   (SEO cannibalization / helpful-content penalty).
3. **Depth over volume.** Target **~25–30 rigorous articles in year one**
   (2–3/month), each verified + cited, not 50 thin ones. Depth is the moat.
4. **Every article follows the GS-00X system** (article + verified references
   + evidence notes + assets) and the publishing gate in README.md.
5. **Honesty is the differentiator.** The Myths cluster is un-copyable by
   supplement marketers — treat it as marquee, not filler.
6. **GS-numbers are permanent IDs, not priority or length.** They file an
   article into a cluster; every article gets the same depth and citation
   rigor regardless of number. **Publish order is a separate axis** (driven by
   search demand — see the launch table). Readers never see the GS-number:
   public URLs are readable slugs (e.g. `/blog/is-my-child-too-short`), not
   `/blog/gs-005`. Numbering blocks: foundation 001–010, hormones 011–020,
   sleep 021–030, nutrition 031–040, activity 041–050, **myths 051–060**,
   puberty 061–070, clinical 071–080.
