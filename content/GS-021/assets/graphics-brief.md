# GS-021 · Graphics Briefs

Four graphics per article. **Brand system:** deep-green `#0E2A20` grounds,
accent `#2F6B4F`, measured-blue `#2A5C8A`, estimated-gold `#9C7A3D`, flag-red
`#A23B3B` (alerts only). Headings Fraunces, body Inter (Sarabun for Thai).
Flat, calm, no fear imagery. Every graphic carries a small "GrowSense · GS-021"
credit and, where a stat appears, its reference number from `references.md`.

## 1. Hero graphic (article header, 1600×900 + 1:1 social crop)
- **Concept:** a child sleeping peacefully in soft, warm early-morning light —
  calm, safe, healthy. Optional subtle motif: a gentle deep-green wave form
  (slow-wave sleep) behind the scene, with one small gold highlight.
- **Headline overlay space:** upper-left kept clean.
- **Tone:** reassurance and rest. No clocks, no anxiety, no clinical imagery.

## 2. The GH–sleep pathway (1:1 + 4:5)
- **Concept:** clean left-to-right (or top-down) flow on the deep-green ground:
  **Sleep onset → deep slow-wave sleep → GH pulse → liver → IGF-1 → growth plate.**
- **Footnote stat:** "~70% of nighttime GH release is during deep sleep — ref 2."
- **Guardrail:** schematic, not anatomical; the point is *indirect support*, not
  "sleep lengthens bone."

## 3. Myth-buster card — "the midnight myth" (1:1 + 4:5)
- **Concept:** two side-by-side sleep timelines, one falling asleep at 10 pm and
  one at midnight — **same GH pulse shape**, just shifted. A struck-through
  "before midnight only."
- **Headline:** "Growth hormone follows sleep onset — not the clock."
- **Footnote:** "ref 1, 3."

## 4. The real signal card — snoring + slow growth (1:1 + 4:5, save-worthy)
- **Concept:** a calm checklist (not alarmist) of OSA red flags — loud nightly
  snoring · breathing pauses / gasping · mouth breathing · restless, sweaty
  sleep · daytime tiredness — with a bottom band: "Snoring **+** slowing growth →
  see a doctor."
- **Footnote:** "Sleep apnea can impair growth; treatment can help — ref 12, 13."
- **Guardrail:** framed as empowerment, not fear; red used only for the checklist
  ticks.

## Production notes
- Provide each in light + dark variants where used on web.
- Thai versions use Sarabun and localized copy from the translation pass.
- Never overstate: no "+X cm" claims; no image implying a wearable can diagnose
  a growth problem.
