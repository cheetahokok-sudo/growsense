# GS-021 · Instagram Carousel (10 slides)

Format 4:5 (1080×1350). Voice: calm, reassuring, myth-busting. The job is to
lower bedtime anxiety while flagging the one real signal (snoring + slow growth).
Stat slides carry a small "ref N" (numbers match `references.md`).

---

**Slide 1 — Hook**
"He went to bed late — did we miss the growth-hormone window?"
Almost every parent has thought it.
Here's the honest answer. 🧵
_(GrowSense · the science, minus the fear)_

**Slide 2 — The reassurance up front**
Sleep matters for growth. Deep sleep matters most.
But one late night does **not** cost your child height.
The body is more resilient than the headlines. _(ref 6)_

**Slide 3 — Why the myth exists**
Growth hormone comes in **pulses.**
One of the biggest arrives just after you fall asleep,
during deep sleep. _(ref 1, 3)_
That's the real kernel of truth.

**Slide 4 — The midnight myth ❌**
"Growth hormone is only released before midnight."
Not true.
The pulse follows **sleep onset — not the clock.** _(ref 1, 3)_
Fall asleep later, the pulse shifts later.

**Slide 5 — One bad night?**
Miss an hour of sleep tonight?
The body makes up the growth hormone **the next day.** _(ref 6)_
Growth happens over months and years — not single nights.

**Slide 6 — Does more sleep = taller?**
No reliable "extra hour → extra centimetres" effect. _(ref 10, 11)_
Enough, regular sleep supports the system.
It doesn't override genetics.

**Slide 7 — Where it REALLY matters** _(save this)_
The one place sleep truly affects growth:
**obstructive sleep apnea** (often big tonsils/adenoids). _(ref 12)_
Watch for loud nightly snoring + slowing growth.

**Slide 8 — The good news**
Treating airway obstruction (adenotonsillectomy)
can bring **catch-up growth** and higher IGF-1. _(ref 13, 22)_
It doesn't add height — it removes the obstacle.

**Slide 9 — Your watch can't tell you**
Fitbit / Apple Watch / Oura estimate sleep stages —
they do **not** measure growth hormone.
A low "deep sleep score" ≠ your child didn't grow.

**Slide 10 — Soft CTA**
Don't chase perfect sleep. Aim for **enough + regular** —
and check with a doctor if snoring meets slowing growth.
GrowSense tracks the pattern, not one night.
