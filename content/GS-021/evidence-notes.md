## Framing guardrails honoured
- **No "sleep makes children taller" claim.** Throughout, sleep is framed as a
  *supporting condition*; §7 explicitly says extra sleep does not add
  centimetres, and §9 frames surgery as *removing an obstacle*, not adding height.
- **Evidence honestly tiered.** The GH–sleep and OSA findings are labelled
  **strong**; the chronic-poor-sleep environment claim is **moderate**; the four
  popular myths are labelled **not supported** (§16).
- **The reassurance is the point.** The strongest practical message — one late
  night is fine; the real signal is nightly snoring + slowing growth — is carried
  by refs 6 (compensation) and 12–22 (SDB), not asserted.

## §12 wearables — deliberately not a research citation
The statement "wearables don't measure growth hormone" is a factual limitation
of the devices (Fitbit/Apple Watch/Oura/WHOOP estimate sleep stages from
motion/HR, not endocrine assays), not a claim needing a PubMed source. Ties to
the app's own wearables integration ([[wearables-architecture]]).
