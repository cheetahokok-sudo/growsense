## Thesis
Children don't stop growing on a birthday — they stop when the **growth
plates fuse**, a process **estrogen drives in both sexes** (in boys via
aromatase conversion of testosterone). So the useful question is not "how
old?" but **"how much runway is left?"** Bone age is the single strongest
routine predictor of that runway, but **not the whole story**: velocity,
pubertal stage and genetics complete it. On-brand: reframes anxiety
("is there still time?") into an honest, multi-signal answer.

## Load-bearing claims → refs
- Growth ends at plate fusion; height made by endochondral ossification → r1, r2.
- Growth plate is a zoned living organ; puberty exhausts the proliferative
  reserve → r1.
- **Estrogen (not testosterone/GH) closes the plate in both sexes**; boys via
  aromatase; aromatase deficiency / AI evidence → r3, r4, r5, r6.
- Puberty paradox: GH/IGF-1 speed growth up while estrogen shortens its
  duration → r13, r14, r3.
- Bone age = biological age; two same-age children, opposite runways → r7, r8.
- Reading methods (GP vs TW), population dependence → r9, r10, r7; caveat r8.
- AI matches expert readers; confirmed by review/meta-analysis → r11, r12.
- Growth velocity, direction > position, <2 cm/yr late puberty ≈ fusing → r14.
- Pubertal-stage runway (girls ~5–7 cm post-menarche; boys later/taller) → r14, r13.
- Heritability ≈ 60–80% sets the ceiling → r15.

## Honesty choices
- **Serial bone age** framed as the differentiator, but not overclaimed —
  presented as "trajectory > single reading," not a proprietary formula.
- The draft's "GrowSense Runway Model" weight table (bone age 30% / velocity
  25% / …) was **cut as pseudo-precise**. Folded into the CTA as "brings the
  signals together, measured vs estimated" — no invented percentages.
- Dropped the draft's specific AI claim ("MAE < 4 months") — not in the
  verified refs; replaced with the sourced "matches expert readers" (r11, r12).
- No "increase height" claim: habits/tests read the runway, they don't extend it.
